AEM Infrastructure and Personnel Strategy: A talk with Tom Johnson of Hirobe

A high-value conversation on a core modernization tension: how infrastructure direction and staffing reality must be designed together to deliver durable AEM outcomes.

OpsInventor • Adobe Experience Manager Consulting & Knowhow

"Should every AEM environment be in the cloud?" is not only an infrastructure question. It is a capability question, an operating model question, and ultimately a sequencing question.

I got a chance to do a fun talk with Tom Johnson, a Senior AEM Recruiter at Hirobe, on the significance of personnel in the major decision points on where you take your AEM projects from an infrastructure standpoint.

Consultancy interpretation Treat cloud adoption, team staffing, and delivery governance as one interconnected strategy system. Optimizing one in isolation often creates downstream drag.

The practical implication is that platform decisions must be paced to your team's current skill profile and realistic hiring/training trajectory. This framing reduces brittle transformation plans and improves execution resilience.

For leaders evaluating AEM as a Cloud Service, the most useful question becomes: "Which capability model allows us to ship confidently over the next 12-24 months?" This turns the conversation from technology preference to delivery economics.

From insight to action

  1. Assess current infra posture and critical delivery constraints.
  2. Map staffing options: internal build, partner blend, targeted hires.
  3. Sequence cloud decisions against capability maturity milestones.
