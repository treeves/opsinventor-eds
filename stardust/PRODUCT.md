# PRODUCT — Tad Reeves (OpsInventor)

## What this site is
The personal site of **Tad Reeves** — Adobe Experience Manager Champion, featured speaker, and Principal Architect with 15+ years in the Adobe ecosystem. **OpsInventor** is the imprint Tad publishes under.

## Primary purposes (in order)
1. **Establish credentials.** AEM Champion, featured conference speaker, deep Principal-Architect-grade experience.
2. **Circulate helpful information.** High-quality, no-fluff content on AEM and the broader Adobe stack — written for engineering leaders and architects.
3. **Encourage contact for hard Adobe architecture problems.** The funnel goal is: "send me your hardest Adobe problem."

## Audience
- Engineering leaders, directors, principal/staff engineers, architects working in the Adobe ecosystem (AEM 6.5, AEMaaCS, Edge Delivery, Adobe Analytics, AEP, etc.).
- People who need real answers, not slideware.

## Home page content pillars
1. **Who is Tad** — credentials, photo/portrait, AEM Champion badge, 15+ year story.
2. **The Rapid Drop** — upcoming video shorts series (60-second AEM/martech term definitions). Multi-platform: YouTube / Instagram / LinkedIn / TikTok. Currently a "launching soon" teaser with subscribe.
3. **Recent speaking engagements** — conference talks and meetups with YouTube thumbnails (adaptTo(), AEM Columbus User Group, Arbory Digital meetups).
4. **Latest insights** — long-form articles (Hirobe interview, SSL on AEM 6.5, in-house vs. managed services).
5. **Direct contact CTA** — "Bring me your hardest Adobe problem."

## Voice and tone
- Progressive, intense, kinetic, confident.
- Verve, interest, competence.
- **Not** bland, staid, conservative, or corporate.
- First person ("I", "me") for personal credibility; "we" only in the context of advisory engagements.

## Visual motif
- Mountains and mountain biking — Tad's known personal brand. Used in hero imagery, section accents, and editorial photography.
- Hard, decisive treatment (no soft gradients) — matches the personality.

## Brand lockup
- Primary: `TAD REEVES`
- Imprint tag: `// OPSINVENTOR`
- Used together in nav and footer.

## What this site is NOT
- Not a corporate consultancy homepage.
- Not a blog index.
- Not a portfolio of past employers.
- Not a generic "thought leadership" site.
