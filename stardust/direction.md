# Direction — Tad Reeves / OpsInventor (Revision 3)

## What changed in this revision
- Positioning pivot from "OpsInventor advanced consultancy" to **Tad Reeves personal brand**, with OpsInventor as the publishing imprint.
- Tone mandate from the principal: **"progressive, intense and show verve, interest and competence. It should not be bland, staid, conservative and corporate."**
- Visual motif: **mountains and mountain biking** — the imagery Tad is known for.
- Variant convergence: **EDGE** chosen as the structural basis. The DEPLOY and SIGNAL variants are deprecated.
- Three new home blocks added: (1) Who is Tad credentials hero, (2) The Rapid Drop teaser, (3) Recent speaking engagements grid.

## Direction phrase
**Tad Reeves, in person, on the page.** Hard-edge, kinetic, mountain-built. Personal credibility plus deep technical authority — no consultancy gloss.

## Audience register
Speaking *as* Tad to engineering leaders, principal engineers, and architects. First person. Direct. Confident. Technical when it matters.

## Visual contract (locked)
- **Type**: Inter (body/UI), Archivo Black (display), Space Mono (eyebrows/meta). **No serifs.**
- **Accent**: `#CE5B12` orange applied as **hard blocks, fills, borders** — never as a gradient blend.
- **Frame**: `#0B1115` ink. Hard splits over centered compositions.
- **Imagery**: mountain / MTB photography with strong dark overlays. Duotone (ink × orange) acceptable for editorial moments.
- **Shape**: `border-radius: 0` everywhere. 3–6px hard accent bars for section markers.

## Content pillars (home)
1. **Who is Tad** — AEM Champion, Featured Speaker, Principal Architect 15+ yrs.
2. **The Rapid Drop** — coming-soon teaser for 60-second AEM/martech term shorts (YT/IG/LI/TikTok).
3. **Recent speaking** — adaptTo() and AEM Columbus User Group / Arbory Digital meetups.
4. **Latest insights** — long-form articles.
5. **CTA** — "Bring me your hardest Adobe problem."

## Brand lockup
- Primary: `TAD REEVES`
- Imprint tag: `// OPSINVENTOR`

## What this direction is NOT
- Not a corporate consultancy homepage.
- Not blog-index-first.
- Not restrained, intelligent, conservative. (That was Revision 2 — explicitly rejected.)
- Not soft-gradient or pastel.
